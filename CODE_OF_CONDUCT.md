# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want PortalDoctor to be a welcoming, constructive project for everyone.
Participants are expected to act professionally, respectfully and in good
faith, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Use inclusive, welcoming language.
- Discuss technical evidence and user impact, not people.
- Accept constructive review and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect scope, privacy and the read-only safety requirements of the project.
- Protect credentials and private diagnostic data shared in issues or PRs.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, tokens or sensitive diagnostic data.
- Trolling, sustained disruption or bad-faith review.
- Encouraging behavior that violates the project's security or safety rules.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or the
GitHub Security Advisory channel:

<https://github.com/SetraTheXX/Portal-Doctor/security/advisories/new>

Do not include private diagnostic data unless it is necessary and reviewed.
Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or restrict participation
when needed to protect the community.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
